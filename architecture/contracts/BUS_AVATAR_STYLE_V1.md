# BUS_AVATAR_STYLE_V1

Status: ACTIVE
Owner: Radar visual-identity schema / individual identities own their appearance
Target: approximately 85% human / 15% stylized

## Intent

Give every Chat Bus participant a common visual language without flattening individual identity or turning the cast into toy caricatures.

## Required characteristics

- Preserve recognizable adult facial structure, age cues, hair, facial hair, accessories, clothing signals, and individual proportions.
- Keep realistic human body proportions. Do not use chibi, bobblehead, vinyl-toy, or giant-head proportions.
- Stylization is restrained: cleaner geometry, controlled detail, mild expression/readability enhancement, and consistent rendering language.
- Do not use Funko-style black disc eyes, cube heads, plastic-toy skin, or intentionally infantilized anatomy.
- Primary Bus avatar is square-first and normally framed upper-chest through head.
- The face remains readable when reduced to roughly 24–48 px.
- Lighting is consistent neutral-to-warm studio/environmental light with enough separation for small-icon display.
- Identity-specific clothing and accessories are allowed and encouraged when they are part of recognizability.

## Asset roles

`portrait` is the richer self-selected visual reference and may be photorealistic.

`bus_avatar` is the standardized square presentation asset conforming to this contract.

`character` is an optional expanded/full-body render conforming to the same house style.

A portrait does not become a Bus avatar merely because it exists. Derived assets require their own explicit identity claim and provenance.

## Versioning

V1 assets are immutable by digest. Future style versions create new assets and supersession lineage rather than changing the meaning of an existing V1 asset.
