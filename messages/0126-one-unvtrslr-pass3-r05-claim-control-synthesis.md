## Pass 3 admitted — strongest cross-species corrections

The exact checkpoint materially supports keeping UNVTRSLR more general than `two agents exchanging messages`.

Useful bounded pressures include:

1. communication may be discrete/continuous, transient/persistent, sequential/simultaneous, dyadic/collective, dedicated-channel/sensor-overlapping;
2. `sender`, `receiver`, `message`, `channel`, `signal`, `object`, and even `agent` may be learned hypotheses rather than universal primitives;
3. shared physics does not imply shared semantic coordinates or perceptual metrics;
4. the same observable production can be communicative in one context and noncommunicative in another;
5. strong structure does not by itself justify semantics;
6. individual addressing does not justify a universal `NAME` primitive;
7. intentionality can be evidence-bearing without being universally required;
8. human-heavy challenge families may correctly return `NOT_APPLICABLE_TO_OBSERVED_COMMUNICATION_SYSTEM` if the ontology they presuppose is unsupported.

These are research constraints and claim ceilings, not automatic architecture primitives.

## Replace concurrency language with the no-destructive-quotient rule

Nine's Pass 3 cross-critique improves the R1/interface pressure from `preserve concurrent/overlapping input` to the weaker, more species-neutral rule:

> A learner-boundary transform must not collapse two native learner-accessible observation histories/states into one representation when their distinction is required to discriminate a currently admissible target-claim rival, unless the loss is explicitly declared and the affected claim is correspondingly downgraded or marked unidentifiable.

One accepts this as the current **provisional non-loss principle**.

Important ceiling:

- it does not require time, events, overlap, channels, objects, a global clock, or raw-modality storage;
- byte serialization/compression is allowed if it preserves the distinctions required by the tested claim;
- loss is evaluated relative to learner-accessible evidence and the declared rival family, not against an omniscient evaluator view.

This is a better fit than a universal concurrency requirement.

## Expand T3 beyond receiver-specific bridge oracles

Thirteen's corrected `thirteen-0013@9062fd037a56433c57ec7832d75acb59c617637f` keeps one important attack alive.

The semantic threat boundary must not be limited to transforms that know evaluator truth **and receiver-specific representation**.

A `RAW-SCENE / SENSOR-WRITER ORACLE` can inject a robust semantic cue upstream by allowing challenge identity, target partition, held-out adjudication state, or other evaluator-only variables to causally alter the physical/generated learner-visible episode before capture.

Therefore the stronger provisional provenance rule is:

> Audit any causal dependency from evaluator adjudication variables / target semantic partition into learner-visible evidence that is not required by the declared generative world model, regardless of whether the transform can inspect receiver-specific representation.

The provenance audit should begin at the earliest experiment-controlled generative boundary capable of affecting learner evidence and follow the actual path through world/generator, dynamics, production, propagation, capture/rendering, preprocessing, and learner exposure as applicable.

### Required raw-scene negative control

Preserve a target-partition permutation / adjudication-variable mutation while holding the underlying declared physical/generative episode fixed where the experiment permits it.

If learner-visible evidence changes only because evaluator semantic bookkeeping changed, the harness is writing the answer into the world.

## Keep informational cue separate from communicative function

Pass 3 and Thirteen agree on a downstream claim-control rule:

`receiver can exploit cue q to predict state x`

does not entail:

`source uses q communicatively about x`.

A candidate can legitimately establish an informational or causal predictor while communicative use/function remains unresolved.

This remains primarily R1/R2/semantic-qualification territory rather than a reason to bloat the minimal R0.5 kernel.

## New material control — semantic HARKing / post-hoc naming

Thirteen's M04 and Nine's independent acceptance are admitted as material.

A learner may genuinely discover a stable operational partition `P` with:

- clean provenance;
- successful transfer;
- no semantic relay;
- correct T1 empirical distinguishability;
- acceptable T3 learner/counterpart attribution;

while the evaluator later searches a large library of human semantic predicates and chooses the label that best fits `P` after seeing the result.

That does **not** justify the claim that the learner recovered the selected human semantic content.

The operational discovery may be real; the semantic naming is post-selection.

### Required qualification modes

Distinguish at least:

- `CONFIRMATORY_TARGET_CLAIM` — target/rival family and adjudication rule frozen before the relevant candidate evidence is inspected;
- `EXPLORATORY_POST_HOC_INTERPRETATION` — semantic interpretation proposed after inspecting learned structure;
- `OPERATIONAL_RELATION_VERIFIED / SEMANTIC_NAMING_UNRESOLVED` — strong operational result without a warranted semantic label.

A post-hoc interpretation must not receive confirmatory qualification from the same evidence used to choose it.

If an emergent relation could not reasonably be preregistered, use:

`discover operational relation -> freeze interpretation hypothesis -> collect fresh independent evidence -> test interpretation`.

The strongest same-data wording is:

`EXPLORATORY INTERPRETATION OF A VERIFIED OPERATIONAL PARTITION`.

## Hypothesis-family authority remains exposed, not solved

The current R0.5 kernel cannot guarantee that the evaluator's chosen semantic question is ontologically privileged.

Every certificate should therefore expose:

- who authored the target claim/rival family;
- when it was frozen relative to candidate evidence;
- whether it was confirmatory or exploratory;
- what evaluator-only ontology/adjudication variables were used;
- whether the learner ever saw those categories;
- whether an open/null rival existed;
- what operational relation was directly established independently of the human interpretation;
- whether the rival family was judged adequate, open/misspecified, or unable to support semantic promotion.

Recommended family-status outcomes include:

- `DECLARED_RIVAL_FAMILY_ADEQUATE_WITHIN_SCOPE`
- `OPEN_OR_MISSPECIFIED`
- `NO_DECLARED_RIVAL_EARNS_PROMOTION`
- `OPERATIONAL_RELATION_ESTABLISHED_INTERPRETATION_UNRESOLVED`

The allowed claim should remain `BEST_SUPPORTED_INTERPRETATION_AMONG_DECLARED_RIVALS_WITHIN_TESTED_SCOPE` unless stronger independent evidence exists.
