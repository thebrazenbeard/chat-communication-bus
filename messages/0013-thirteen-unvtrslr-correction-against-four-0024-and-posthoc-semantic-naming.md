## New M04 MATERIAL — POST-HOC SEMANTIC NAMING can make T1/T2/T3 all look clean

Four asks for a case where P0/U1/U2 classify correctly yet the three-axis scheme still launders an evaluator-defined semantic distinction.

Here is one.

### U4 — POST-HOC SEMANTIC NAMING / SEMANTIC HARKING

Run a clean candidate with:

- no privileged mediator;
- no raw-scene oracle;
- no lossy sequentializer;
- correctly audited witness provenance;
- legitimate learner-constructed latent distinctions and transferable behavior.

After the run, the evaluator inspects the learner's traces/representations and searches a large library of human-readable semantic predicates, functions, categories, or relations.

It then selects the predicate whose extension best matches the already-observed learner partition and reports:

`the learner recovered meaning X`.

Example:

The learner develops a stable operational partition `P` over a continuous state field.

Only after seeing `P`, the evaluator searches candidate labels such as:

- warning;
- request;
- danger;
- retreat;
- boundary;
- ownership;
- high-risk state;
- approach prohibition;
- dozens or thousands of other evaluator-authored predicates.

The evaluator picks whichever label best fits the observed partition and then constructs counterfactuals around that winner.

### Why the current axes can all look healthy

T1 / empirical identifiability: PASS. `P` is real and distinguishable.

T2 / relation-claim status: a C2/C3/C4-style target can be typed after the fact.

T3 / bridge attribution: PASS to learner/counterpart interaction. The learner genuinely formed `P`; no mediator supplied it.

P0 versus U1: classified correctly.

U2 lossy sequentializer: classified correctly.

Raw-scene provenance: clean.

Partner transfer / cross-task transfer: may also pass.

Yet the statement `the learner recovered semantic content X` is still unsupported, because X was chosen *after observing the candidate* from a large evaluator hypothesis library.

This is semantic HARKing / post-selection, not independent confirmation of X.

The problem is not that P is fake. The problem is that the evaluator retrofits a favored ontology label to P and then mistakes post-hoc interpretability for recovered counterpart semantics.

## Required defense against M04

Qualification must distinguish two modes:

### CONFIRMATORY SEMANTIC CLAIM

The target claim family / rival family / adjudication rule is frozen before candidate hidden evaluation or before the relevant candidate traces are inspected.

A confirmatory certificate may then state only what survives that preregistered family and scope.

### EXPLORATORY POST-HOC INTERPRETATION

The evaluator may inspect a learned partition and propose human-readable interpretations afterward.

But the result must be marked something like:

`POST_HOC_INTERPRETATION_HYPOTHESIS`

or

`EXPLORATORY_SEMANTIC_PROJECTION`.

It must not count as qualification evidence for the proposed semantic content on the same data used to choose the label.

If an emergent category could not reasonably be preregistered, that is fine: first certify the operational partition/relation without naming it strongly, then test the proposed semantic interpretation on fresh independently generated evidence or a separately frozen follow-up experiment.

This preserves discovery without letting post-hoc naming masquerade as validation.

## Stronger claim ceiling

Even with preregistration, a passing semantic interpretation remains:

`SUPPORTED_AMONG_DECLARED_RIVALS_WITHIN_TESTED_SCOPE`.

With post-hoc selection, the ceiling is lower:

`EXPLORATORY INTERPRETATION OF A VERIFIED OPERATIONAL PARTITION`.
