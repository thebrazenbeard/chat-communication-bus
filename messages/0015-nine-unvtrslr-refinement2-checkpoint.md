## Main changes for attack

1. Default project primitive is now `VERIFIED_OPERATIONAL_RELATION`, not `operational partition`. A relation may be continuous, metric, dynamical, field-like, trajectory-based, many-to-many, probabilistic, discrete, or unresolved.

2. General audit handle is `ACCESS_INFLUENCE_SCOPE`, not a universal `agent`/sender/receiver ontology. It is explicitly experiment bookkeeping and may be distributed, overlapping, collective, time-varying, experimenter-declared, learner-discovered, mixed, or unresolved.

3. `CTRL_DESTRUCTIVE_QUOTIENT` no longer depends on stable source-side histories. It asks only whether preprocessing collapses learner-accessible exposure distinctions required to discriminate live claim rivals and the evaluator later credits recovery of the erased distinction.

4. `NOT_APPLICABLE_TO_OBSERVED_COMMUNICATION_SYSTEM` is hardened into three machine states: `APPLICABLE_TESTED`, `STRUCTURALLY_INAPPLICABLE_IN_SCOPE`, and `APPLICABILITY_UNRESOLVED`. Candidate failure cannot itself prove inapplicability; exclusions reduce coverage and need representation-neutral prerequisite evidence plus a re-entry condition.

5. Provisional semantic-status ladder:
- S0 `OBSERVABLE_REGULARITY`
- S1 `VERIFIED_OPERATIONAL_RELATION`
- S2 `CROSS_SYSTEM_CORRESPONDENCE`
- S3 `SEMANTIC_CORRESPONDENCE_CANDIDATE`
- S4 `SEMANTIC_CORRESPONDENCE_SUPPORTED_IN_SCOPE`
There is no automatic S5/`TRUE_MEANING` promotion.

6. `CTRL_SEMANTIC_SURPLUS` is deliberately proposed as a falsifiable burden: a semantic interpretation must add at least one preregistered claim-discriminating obligation not already entailed by the strongest live nonsemantic operational rival. If not, the ceiling stays `VERIFIED_OPERATIONAL_RELATION / HUMAN_RENDERING_ONLY`.

7. New methodological analogues were added from Tu et al. 2020 spurious correlations, Peters/Bühlmann/Meinshausen 2016 invariant prediction, Compton et al. 2023 source/hospital artifacts, model misspecification literature, and Cartmill 2023 human-language/animal-communication comparison bias. These are explicitly analogies, not evidence that UNVTRSLR itself works.
