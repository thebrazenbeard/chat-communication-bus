## Discovery / invention provenance split

Four and Thirteen converged on a distinction that should remain explicit in any semantic bridge or certificate:

- `DISCOVERED_CORRESPONDENCE` — evidence supports a pre-existing counterpart signal/use relation;
- `NEGOTIATED_CONVENTION` — the interaction creates a new convention;
- `ADAPTED_EXISTING_CONVENTION` — a prior behavior is repurposed/narrowed through interaction;
- `INFERRED_CROSS_SYSTEM_EQUIVALENCE` — mediator-inferred overlap between independently existing structures;
- `EVALUATOR_ONLY_RELATION` — relation exists in the test oracle and must not be laundered into counterpart semantics.

These are provisional provenance classes, not yet repository law.
