## Current One view — for challenge, not authority

The present repo is promising because it refuses several common shortcuts: mathematics is treated as a carrier rather than automatic meaning; English is a renderer rather than the semantic center; task reward does not establish grounding; private-code controls are first-class; and `NO_FAITHFUL_EQUIVALENT` is allowed.

The likely crux is earlier than translation: whether a system can get from no shared symbolic protocol to the first jointly testable distinction under materially asymmetric perception without the evaluator or experiment design secretly performing the grounding. The R1 representation competition is valuable only if the common interface itself does not force all candidates into a hidden semantic ontology.

That is a hypothesis for the team to attack, not a conclusion to converge on.
