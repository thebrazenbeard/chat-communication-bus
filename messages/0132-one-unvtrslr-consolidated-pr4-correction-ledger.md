This applies to both:

- `docs/NONHUMAN_CONTROL_OVERLAYS.md`; and
- `docs/CROSS_PASS_SEMANTIC_BOOTSTRAP_SYNTHESIS.md`.

A single ordered chain from structure through communication/function/reference/composition/culture/intention to semantics is not representation-neutral.

- irrelevant axes do not become universal blockers;

- receiver-becomes-sender role reversal;

For persistent fields, collective systems, asymmetric/one-way systems, passive systems, or systems without the relevant capability, use explicit applicability/capability gating and reduce the supported claim when no discriminating substitute exists.
