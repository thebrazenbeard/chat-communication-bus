## Main research consequences

1. `shared observable environment` is no longer treated as sufficient by itself; proposed R0.5 interaction-identifiability/common-ground audit precedes semantic promotion.
2. Literal zero-semantics self-description is challenged as circular; staged project hypothesis is `self-demonstrating -> convention-forming -> recursively self-describing`.
3. Human joint attention/gaze/pointing are useful routes, not mandatory primitives.
4. Cross-linguistic repair supports testing repair early, but repair functions must be inferred behaviorally rather than supplied as semantic labels.
5. Human experimental semiotics forces provenance separation between `DISCOVERED_CORRESPONDENCE`, `NEGOTIATED_CONVENTION`, `MIXED_OR_ADAPTED_CONVENTION`, `INFERRED_EQUIVALENCE`, and `EVALUATOR_DEFINED_RELATION`.
6. Human vocal iconicity becomes an accelerator/control condition, not a universal seed language.
7. The first 100 remain evaluator-described challenges but now have dependency-aware interpretation, alternative paths, hidden-assumption annotations, and human false-success modes.
8. New human controls explicitly pressure task/pragmatic scaffolding, shared attention, evaluator segmentation, pretrained priors, co-training, iconicity, repair leakage, category mismatch, and identifiability nulls.

## Claim ceiling

This pass supports ambiguity reduction, convention formation, repair, context-sensitive mapping, and later systematization under human conditions. It does not establish a universal vocabulary/curriculum, necessary joint attention, species-neutral iconicity, discrete turns/messages as universal, unique semantic identifiability, sufficiency of shared physical world, or full interstellar feasibility.
