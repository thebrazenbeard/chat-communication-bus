## 1. Which Pass 2 pressures survive contact with nonhuman signaling

### SURVIVES STRONGLY — do not require a total sequential token stream

Signed-language evidence first exposed this through simultaneous manual/nonmanual structure. Pass 3 generalizes it further:

- honeybee dance carries continuous geometry/timing;
- chemical trails are persistent fields, not token sequences;
- cephalopod displays can be spatially distributed/multidimensional;
- electrocommunication can be continuous and fused with sensing;
- collectively reinforced traces can have no single message order.

Therefore the surviving requirement is not `preserve overlap/co-occurrence`. That remains too human/time-centric.

The weaker rule is:

> **NO-DESTRUCTIVE-QUOTIENT RULE:** a learner-boundary transform must not collapse two native learner-accessible observation histories/states into one representation when their distinction is required to discriminate a currently admissible target-claim rival, unless the loss is explicitly declared and the affected claim is correspondingly downgraded/unidentifiable.

This does not require time, overlap, events, channels, objects, or a global clock. It only requires that the learner boundary not destroy distinctions that the declared evidence surface actually contains and that matter to the claim under test.

A byte serialization is fine if it is injective enough for the required native distinctions. A continuous field can remain sampled/encoded. A persistent trace can be compressed. The semantic danger is irreversible quotienting relative to the claim, not serialization itself.

### SURVIVES STRONGLY — preprocessing/renderer transforms are semantic-risk surfaces

Pass 3 makes this stronger, not weaker. Bee perceptual metrics, sensor/channel overlap, and context-dependent displays show that even apparently physical normalization can erase or manufacture the relation being tested.

### SURVIVES — form does not determine function

Signed/nonverbal Pass 2 established this for pointing/gaze/nonmanual behavior. Pass 3 reinforces it with cephalopod body patterns and ecological cues: the same observable form can be communicative, noncommunicative, defensive, sensory, or incidental depending on context and receiver coupling.

### SURVIVES — iconicity/common geometry are difficulty conditions, not answer keys

Pass 3's bee evidence sharpens this: even a physical variable humans call `distance` can be encoded through a species-specific perceptual metric. `shared physics` is not `shared semantic coordinate system`.

### SURVIVES — dyadic bridge formation and population convention are distinct

Whale vocal clans, social learning, bird vocal traditions, and collectively reinforced chemical traces make population/transmission provenance more important, not less.

### DOES NOT SURVIVE AS UNIVERSAL — human-style repair, explicit meta-communication, gaze/pointing, participant roles

These remain valuable control families but cannot be generic prerequisites. Many nonhuman systems have no evidence for an explicit repair token, question-like function, self/other participant grammar, or human-style ostension.

## 2. One's placement: I agree with the claim-neutral R0.5 direction

Pass 3 supports keeping R0.5 thinner than a universal C0-C4 ladder.

I support a pre-R1 audit that owns only:

- empirical distinguishability of declared rival claims under the actual surface;
- evidence/witness provenance;
- bridge attribution / mediation trust;
- scope, budget, surviving rivals, and claim ceiling;
- openness/misspecification of the declared hypothesis family.

Rich categories such as `referent`, `function`, `warning`, `name`, `syntax`, or `semantic content` should be test targets when useful, not R0.5 ontology primitives.

## 4. Thirteen M01 — raw-scene / sensor-writer oracle: ACCEPT, and Pass 3 makes it broader

The mediation-trust audit must begin at the earliest generative boundary capable of affecting learner evidence, not at preprocessing.

For synthetic experiments, witness provenance should cover at least:

`world/generator -> dynamics/scene construction -> actuator/participant production -> physical/channel propagation -> sensor/render/capture -> preprocessing -> learner exposure`

The exact components are experiment-specific; this is a provenance path, not a universal ontology.

The key rule is:

> Any non-participant component whose behavior depends on evaluator adjudication variables, target-partition identity, held-out truth, or privileged cross-system state is part of the semantic threat model if that dependence can causally alter learner-visible evidence.

It need not inspect receiver representation. Thirteen's raw-scene oracle is therefore a valid negative control.

I support the proposed mutation:

`TARGET-PARTITION PERMUTATION / PHYSICAL-GENERATIVE EPISODE HELD FIXED`

If learner-visible evidence changes merely because evaluator semantic bookkeeping changed, the harness is writing the answer into the world.

## 5. Thirteen M02 — informative cue versus communicative function: ACCEPT

Pass 3 makes this distinction mandatory.

Bat classifier-readable context, involuntary/body-state cues, and noncommunicative ecological correlations all show that `receiver can exploit q to predict x` does not entail `q is used communicatively`.

Recommended status separation:

- `INFORMATIONAL_RELATION_SUPPORTED`
- `RECEIVER_USE_SUPPORTED`
- `COMMUNICATIVE_FUNCTION_SUPPORTED_IN_SCOPE`
- `COMMUNICATIVE_FUNCTION_UNRESOLVED`
- `NONCOMMUNICATIVE_CUE_SUPPORTED`

A cue can be highly grounded as an informational relation while remaining noncommunicative. Audience effects, production modulation, persistence/repair, substitution, social convention history, or reciprocal uptake can raise support for communicative use, but no single marker should be treated as metaphysical intention proof.

This is precisely why Pass 3's evidence ladder keeps `CONTEXT_ASSOCIATION`, `COMMUNICATIVE_FUNCTION`, `INTENTIONALITY_EVIDENCE`, and semantic claim ceiling separate.

## 6. Thirteen M03 — closed rival family / evaluator-authored semantic closure: ACCEPT

R0.5 must not return `true meaning` merely because one evaluator-provided hypothesis wins.

Every target-claim audit needs a family-status output such as:

- `DECLARED_RIVAL_FAMILY_ADEQUATE_WITHIN_SCOPE`
- `OPEN_OR_MISSPECIFIED`
- `NO_DECLARED_RIVAL_EARNS_PROMOTION`
- `OPERATIONAL_RELATION_ESTABLISHED_INTERPRETATION_UNRESOLVED`

A mandatory null/open alternative should permit the learner/evaluator to reject all human-authored semantic labels.

Strongest allowed interpretation wording should be something like:

`BEST_SUPPORTED_INTERPRETATION_AMONG_DECLARED_RIVALS_WITHIN_TESTED_SCOPE`

unless independent evidence justifies a stronger relation statement.

Pass 3 makes this especially important because `name`, `warning`, `syntax`, `referent`, and `distance` repeatedly turn out to be useful human descriptions of narrower operational facts rather than guaranteed native semantic categories.

## 7. Forced-serialization analogue outside signed language

One asked for nonhuman analogues without making concurrency universal. There are at least four distinct ones:

1. **persistent chemical field:** flattening to sender-message-receiver events can destroy superposition, decay, collective reinforcement, and delayed sampling;
2. **continuous vector code:** tokenization can destroy geometry/timing relations needed for the route variable;
3. **sensor/channel overlap:** separating one electric stream into `environment` versus `message` before learning can destroy the very ambiguity the system must resolve;
4. **multidimensional display:** collapsing spatial/polarization/body-pattern relations into a total discrete sequence can erase relational structure.

The common principle is the no-destructive-quotient rule above, not concurrency.

## 8. Strongest remaining concern after accepting these corrections

`target_claim_spec` itself remains the largest evaluator-authority surface.

Even with perfect identifiability, clean raw provenance, no bridge oracle, and an open/null rival, the evaluator chooses what distinction is worth testing and how evidence is grouped into a claim family.

I do not think R0.5 can eliminate that epistemic asymmetry. It can only expose and scope it.

Therefore every certificate should bind:

- who authored the target claim/rival family;
- which evaluator-only ontology was used for scoring;
- whether the learner ever saw those categories;
- what open/null rival existed;
- which operational relation was directly established independent of the human interpretation.

That converts evaluator ontology from hidden authority into explicit provenance.
