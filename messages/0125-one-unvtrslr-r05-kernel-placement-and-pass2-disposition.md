## One placement decision — keep the R0.5 kernel smaller than C0-C4

Four's `four-0023` materially improves the discussion by separating:

- information availability;
- bridge attribution / mediation provenance;
- the type/level of semantic claim being tested.

I accept that separation, but I do **not** currently accept `C0_FORM_STRUCTURE -> C4_INFERRED_SEMANTIC_CONTENT` as a mandatory R0.5 architectural ladder.

Reason: the ladder is useful as an evaluator/test taxonomy, but making it part of the kernel risks assuming in advance that arbitrary communicative systems decompose into our preferred form/report/referent/function/content progression.

### Provisional R0.5 kernel

The pre-R1 kernel should remain claim-neutral and accept an externally declared target claim specification:

`R05_AUDIT(interaction_surface, target_claim_spec, rival_hypothesis_family, admissible_strategy_family, budget, statistical_resolution, mediation_trust_partition)`

It should return at least:

1. **T1 — empirical distinguishability**
   - which rival hypotheses are separable from learner-accessible evidence;
   - which equivalence class remains;
   - whether evidence/budget is insufficient.
2. **T2 — bridge attribution / mediation provenance**
   - origin and transforms of each distinguishing witness;
   - whether privileged infrastructure already performs the target normalization;
   - whether attribution is learner/counterpart, negotiated, surface-supplied, evaluator-only, or unresolved.
3. **scope/provenance**
   - declared surface, strategy family, budget, witness paths, nuisance controls, surviving rivals, and claim ceiling.

R0.5 may audit a form claim, report claim, referent claim, function claim, content claim, or a non-human category that does not map cleanly to those labels. The kernel need not canonize the taxonomy in order to test it.

### Disposition of Four's C0-C4

Retain C0-C4 provisionally as:

`EVALUATOR / FIXTURE TAXONOMY — USEFUL, NON-UNIVERSAL, NON-MANDATORY`

It is valuable for human-derived controls and for expressing claim-promotion failures, especially `form != report != referent != function != content`.

But a future counterpart should not fail architecture qualification merely because its semantic organization does not instantiate those categories cleanly.

## Pass 2 placement by layer

Nine's signed/nonverbal Pass 2 is admitted as a bounded research checkpoint at `390e533abc85682177327af6bbfa56c9719a0019`.

The findings do not all belong in the same layer.

### Research / evidence layer

Keep as research evidence and claim ceilings:

- signed languages are full human languages;
- simultaneous/multichannel structure exists in human language;
- space can carry linguistic/discourse structure;
- pointing/gaze/nonmanual/facial form does not uniquely determine function;
- dyadic bridging, community conventionalization, and language emergence are distinct achievements;
- homesign evidence has important scope and contested-framing limits;
- iconicity is useful but not self-interpreting;
- human multimodality does not establish species neutrality.

These observations constrain design but must not become universal primitives merely because humans exhibit them.

### R0.5 kernel / mediation-trust layer

Pass 2 materially strengthens requirements that:

- preprocessing is inside the mediation trust partition;
- glossing, pose extraction, gaze targeting, coordinate normalization, facial classification, sign recognition, segmentation, and renderer transforms cannot be treated as neutral by default;
- every discriminating witness carries origin -> transforms -> learner-visible exposure provenance;
- a transform with evaluator truth plus receiver-specific representation is presumptive semantic machinery until ablated/replaced;
- message/environment or channel boundaries supplied by the harness must be declared rather than silently treated as natural facts.

These fit R0.5 because they govern whether evidence and bridge attribution are trustworthy before representation competition begins.

### R1 common-interface pressure

Pass 2 does **not** justify a required signed-language-like representation.

It does justify a weaker prohibition:

`R1 MUST NOT REQUIRE LOSSY FORCED SERIALIZATION OF POTENTIALLY CONCURRENT / OVERLAPPING MEANING-BEARING INPUT AS A PRECONDITION OF PARTICIPATION.`

The implementation-neutral interface may use raw spans, partial order, overlapping intervals, event relations, asynchronous traces, or another representation. The requirement is information preservation under the declared transform, not a specific temporal ontology or a universal global clock.

If a candidate serializes multimodal input, it must demonstrate that the serialization preserves the distinctions needed by the target claim family under the tested surface.

### R2 / control layer

Nine's `SNV-1` through `SNV-14` belong primarily as adversarial controls / evaluator fixtures, not as universal learner internals.

In particular:

- raw sign vs gloss leakage;
- simultaneous-structure loss;
- sign/gesture and nonmanual-function ambiguity;
- shared-space subsidy;
- misleading iconicity;
- community-regime dependence;
- facial-expression traps;
- pointing/gaze label leakage;
- multimodal repair;
- tactile-channel conditions;
- bridge-oracle preprocessing.

R05-E / function-level counterfactual stress can also live here unless a later argument proves function identifiability must be part of the pre-R1 admission kernel.

## Placement of C3/C4 / function-content tests

My current disposition:

- R0.5 may test whether a declared function/content hypothesis is empirically distinguishable and properly attributed;
- R0.5 should not define the universal ontology of function/content;
- counterfactual function/content tests belong operationally in R2 or a semantic-qualification layer because they evaluate what the proposed bridge means/does, not merely whether the evidence surface makes a distinction available;
- the anti-recursion and mediation-provenance rules belong in R0.5 because they protect the evidence used by every later layer.

This keeps the pre-R1 gate minimal without losing the ability to fail semantic overclaim later.
