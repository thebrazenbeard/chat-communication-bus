## Evidence discipline

Pass 3 uses `vocal` and `non-vocal`, not `verbal/nonverbal`.

The evidence matrix separates:

`STRUCTURE -> RECEIVER_DISCRIMINATION -> COMMUNICATIVE_FUNCTION -> CONTEXT_ASSOCIATION -> REFERENCE_LIKE_EVIDENCE -> COMBINATION -> SOCIAL_LEARNING/CULTURE -> INTENTIONALITY_EVIDENCE -> SEMANTIC_CLAIM_CEILING`

This is an audit ladder, not a claim that every species traverses the same stages.

## Main cross-species pressure

1. Rich signal structure is not semantics. Sperm-whale coda work is the strongest current example: contextual/combinatorial structure is real while semantic functions remain substantially unresolved.
2. A shared physical world does not imply a shared represented variable. Honeybee dance distance is tied to bee optic-flow odometry, so evaluator meters cannot be silently promoted into bee semantics.
3. Individual addressing is not one universal `NAME` primitive. Dolphins, parrots, and elephants show different mechanisms.
4. Combination is not automatically syntax or compositional meaning. Structural combination, receiver sensitivity, predictable functional combination, and semantic generalization are distinct evidence levels.
5. Intentionality can provide strong evidence where operationally demonstrated, as in great-ape gesture, but it cannot be a universal prerequisite.
6. Communication can persist in the environment and be collectively authored, as in pheromone trails; one sender/one receiver/discrete message cannot be architecture law.
7. Communication and sensing can share a physical substrate, as in electrocommunication/electrolocation.
8. The same actuator/pattern family can be communicative and noncommunicative across contexts, so signalhood must remain contextual.
9. Channel discovery precedes semantics; chemical, electric, vibrational, polarization-sensitive, tactile, and persistent channels can fail at sensing before any semantic decoder is relevant.

## Thesis change

The two-agent shared-environment fixture remains the first controlled experiment, but the general theory is now narrower about what it can assume and broader about possible communication form.

Current thesis candidate:

> Under declared conditions where an interaction surface contains recoverable distinguishing structure and no privileged mediator supplies the target correspondence, initially unaligned systems may be able to construct scoped, testable, reusable cross-system semantic correspondences without a pre-shared symbolic language.

Generalized subject candidate:

> two or more initially unaligned adaptive systems or coupled processes with partially overlapping observables and influence surfaces.

`agent`, `sender`, `receiver`, `message`, `channel`, `object`, and `signal` may be earned hypotheses rather than universal primitives.
