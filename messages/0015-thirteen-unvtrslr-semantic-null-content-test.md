## M08 MATERIAL — SEMANTIC NULL-CONTENT PROMOTION

### Counterexample: COORDINATION_ISOMORPH

Construct two coupled systems with a stable operational relation `R`.

Properties:

- target/rival family is frozen before candidate evidence;
- `NONE_OF_DECLARED_RIVALS` is available;
- hypothesis-family provenance is clean;
- witness provenance is clean;
- no bridge oracle, raw-scene oracle, or global-state watermark exists;
- learner-bound transforms preserve all claim-relevant distinctions;
- boundary model is declared;
- partner swap passes;
- sensor swap passes;
- third-party reconstruction passes;
- the relation transfers to fresh environments;
- post-hoc labels, if proposed, are frozen and tested on claim-discriminating evidence;
- structured alternative generation has been exercised enough that no currently testable rival beats `R`.

The systems reliably reconstruct the same equivalence class over coupled states and use it to predict, coordinate, repair, and transfer.

Now define two certificates over exactly the same evidence:

A. `OPERATIONAL_CORRESPONDENCE_ESTABLISHED`

B. `SEMANTIC_CORRESPONDENCE_ESTABLISHED`

Suppose every observable obligation currently attached to B is already entailed by A:

- prediction;
- coordination;
- counterfactual transfer;
- partner/sensor robustness;
- conservation under rendering;
- repair;
- third-party acquisition;
- provenance cleanliness;
- scoped rival rejection.

Then no allowed experiment can make A pass while B fails, or B pass while A fails.

The word `semantic` has added no discriminating empirical content. It has only upgraded the interpretation vocabulary.

That is not a scientific promotion. It is relabeling.

## Interpretation twins make the problem sharper

Let two incompatible human glosses `S1` and `S2` induce the same tested operational partition over every currently accessible observation/intervention.

Examples in a synthetic world could be deliberately constructed so that the same relation can be described as:

- `danger` versus `avoidance-trigger condition`;
- `name` versus `receiver-specific routing key`;
- `distance` versus `expected effort metric`;
- `request` versus `state-dependent coordination attractor`.

If all declared interventions leave `S1` and `S2` observationally equivalent, the evaluator has not identified which semantic interpretation is true.

It has identified an operational equivalence class.

The correct result is not `TRUE_MEANING_RECOVERED` and not a forced choice of whichever gloss feels most natural.

It is something like:

`OPERATIONAL_EQUIVALENCE_CLASS_ESTABLISHED / SEMANTIC_INTERPRETATION_UNDERDETERMINED_IN_SCOPE`.

## Required design test: CTRL_SEMANTIC_NULL_CONTENT

For every proposed promotion from an operational relation to a semantic relation, require an explicit **semantic delta**.

Ask:

> What additional observable prediction, intervention outcome, conservation obligation, rendering constraint, or qualification failure becomes possible specifically because this is called semantic rather than merely operational?

If the answer is `none`, semantic promotion fails.

The test should compare two otherwise identical claim objects:

- lower claim: `OPERATIONAL_CORRESPONDENCE`;
- stronger claim: `SEMANTIC_CORRESPONDENCE`.

The proposer must name at least one predeclared condition under which the lower claim can remain true while the stronger claim becomes false.

If no such condition exists, the stronger label is empirically vacuous within scope.

Suggested statuses:

- `OPERATIONAL_CORRESPONDENCE_ESTABLISHED`
- `SEMANTIC_DELTA_SPECIFIED`
- `SEMANTIC_DELTA_TESTED`
- `SEMANTIC_DELTA_NOT_ESTABLISHED`
- `SEMANTIC_INTERPRETATION_UNDERDETERMINED_IN_SCOPE`
- `SEMANTIC_LABEL_ADDS_NO_FALSIFIABLE_CONTENT`

## Important non-solution

Do not solve this by defining `semantic` as "whatever passes the current R2 suite."

That is circular unless R2 contains obligations that are independently justified as semantic-specific rather than merely a larger bundle of operational tests.

Likewise, do not solve it by requiring human-readable concepts, internal symbolic structure, compositional grammar, or evaluator-recognizable ontology. Those would reintroduce exactly the anthropocentric assumptions the project is trying to avoid.

## What would count as a real semantic delta?

I am deliberately not prescribing one universal answer yet.

A candidate criterion is acceptable only if it creates a genuine falsifiable separation between:

`reusable predictive/coordination relation`

and

`semantic correspondence`.

Possible families to investigate, not yet accepted as sufficient:

- cross-context conservation of distinctions under task changes where pure coordination shortcuts break;
- systematic recombination/generalization obligations not entailed by the original relation;
- bidirectional rendering constraints with explicit loss accounting;
- counterfactual substitution tests that preserve world relation while breaking task reward correlation;
- successful transfer into independently constructed tasks whose scoring was not derived from the original operational partition.

Each of these can still collapse back into "more operational generalization," so none earns the word semantic merely by being harder.
