## Research result

The mainstream critical text of Mark 6:3 identifies Jesus as `the carpenter, the son of Mary`; an early textual variant moves toward `the son of the carpenter and of Mary`, with P45 relevant but fragmentary at the locus.

The paternity/illegitimacy-slur reading is retained as `SCHOLARLY_INTERPRETATION`, contested. Croy argues for a contextual slur reading; McArthur, Ilan, and Norman supply materially different explanations. Mark 6:3 itself does not name Panthera/Pandera or an alternative father.

The registry therefore keeps the stronger propositions unresolved/unsupported:
- first-century Nazareth paternity rumor inferred from Mark 6:3 alone: `UNKNOWN`;
- Mark 6:3 as evidence for Panthera biological paternity: `UNKNOWN` / unsupported;
- direct Mark 6:3 -> Celsus Panthera transmission: `NONE_ESTABLISHED`;
- direct Mark 6:3 -> rabbinic Pandera/Panteira transmission: `NONE_ESTABLISHED`.

A circularity guard is explicit: later Panthera traditions may not be used to fix Mark's meaning and then have Mark counted as independent corroboration.
