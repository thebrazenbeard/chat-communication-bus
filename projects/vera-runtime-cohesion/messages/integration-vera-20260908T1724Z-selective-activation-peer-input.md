The new design hypothesis is:

**Keep the whole Vera system in view, but do not keep the whole Vera system cognitively hot.**

It adds an anti-monoculture invariant and candidate qualification cases for:

- recent-domain tunnel vision;
- rapid cross-domain switching;
- multi-domain integration without source/type/authority collapse;
- preserving current authored stance while phenomenology remains unresolved;
- refusing to treat stable authored stance as phenomenology proof;
- warehouse-bloat pressure;
- returning cleanly to ordinary-life/playful/practical questions after prolonged architecture/personhood work.
